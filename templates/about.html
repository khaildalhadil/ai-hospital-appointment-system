Problem

Traditional hospital booking by phone or in person leads to long waiting times, scheduling conflicts, missed appointments, slow patient support and a heavy workload for hospital staff.

Objectives

  1. Design a smart hospital appointment booking system.
  2. Develop an AI-powered patient support chatbot.
  3. Provide automated appointment reminders for patients.
  4. Reduce patient waiting time and scheduling conflicts.
  5. Improve communication between patients and hospitals.
  6. Store and manage patient appointment records securely.

How this prototype meets the requirements

RequirementImplementation
Register and log inPatient accounts with hashed passwords (Werkzeug PBKDF2) and role-based access for patients, doctors and admins.
Book, cancel, reschedule onlineBooking only offers free slots generated from each doctor's schedule; a database unique index prevents double booking.
Automatic remindersAppointments in the next 48 hours appear as reminders on the patient dashboard.
Doctor schedules & availabilityAdmins set working days and hours per doctor; available times are calculated live.
AI chatbot supportRule-based natural-language assistant: intent detection, symptom-to-department mapping, and live recommendation of the earliest free slots.
Admin managementManage all appointments and statuses, doctor schedules, new doctors and patient records.
ReportsTotals, 7-day load and breakdowns by department and doctor, printable as PDF.
Doctor use casesDoctors see today's and upcoming appointments and mark them completed, no-show or cancelled.

Technology

Python (Flask) backend, SQLite database (swappable for MySQL), HTML, CSS and JavaScript frontend. Development followed an Agile methodology: planning, analysis, design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance.

Out of scope, as defined in the project: medical diagnosis, online payment and prescription management. The assistant's department suggestions are guidance only, not a diagnosis.
